Caribbean Tender Private Procurement Platform is a Jamaica-based digital procurement solution designed to make business opportunities more accessible, efficient, and inclusive across the Caribbean. We believe that every business, regardless of its size, location, or connections, deserves an opportunity to compete, grow, and succeed.

## Built in the Caribbean. Designed for the Caribbean.

The Caribbean is home to ambitious entrepreneurs, innovative companies, and skilled service providers. Yet opportunities for growth are not always equally accessible.

Caribbean Tender seeks to change how businesses connect with these opportunities.

Starting in Jamaica, our ambition is to develop a regional procurement network that allows businesses to reach new markets, build valuable partnerships, and compete for opportunities beyond geographical boundaries.

## Who We Are

Caribbean Tender was founded on a simple belief: Business opportunities should be determined by capability, not connections.

Across Jamaica and the wider Caribbean, many SMEs face challenges accessing contracts. Opportunities are often circulated through established networks, limiting exposure for emerging businesses and reducing the pool of potential suppliers available to buyers.

Caribbean Tender is being developed to bridge this gap.

Through a centralized digital platform, we aim to connect businesses seeking goods and services with suppliers and service providers ready to deliver, creating a more accessible and competitive procurement environment.

## What We Do

We facilitate ACCESS for all.

**For Buyers:** Businesses will be able to advertise procurement opportunities, invite competitive bids, discover qualified suppliers, and manage tender submissions more efficiently.

**For Suppliers:** Entrepreneurs, contractors, service providers, and established businesses will be able to discover private-sector opportunities, submit proposals, and expand their reach beyond traditional business networks.

## Our Mission

To expand access to procurement opportunities in the private sector by connecting buyers and suppliers through an accessible, efficient, and user-friendly digital platform.

## What Drives Us

Our platform is being built around four (4) guiding principles:

- Accessibility
- Efficiency
- Integrity
- Regional Integration

We are building more than a procurement platform. We are creating a gateway to business opportunities across the Caribbean.
